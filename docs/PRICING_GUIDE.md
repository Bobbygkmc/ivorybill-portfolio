# Pricing Guide

Estimated ranges for common engagements. Final quotes depend on scope,
urgency, hardware and software costs, site conditions, and project
complexity. This document is the source of truth for the website's
`pricing.html` page — update here first, then mirror to the page.

> **Disclaimer**: Prices below are estimates only. They are not a
> binding quote. Hardware and software (licenses, subscriptions,
> cameras, switches, cabling, hosting, domains) are billed separately
> and itemized in any written proposal.

---

## AI and automation

| Service                                  | Estimated price   | Typical timeframe       |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| AI workflow setup (intake, summaries)    | $300 – $2,500+    | 2 days to 3 weeks       |
| Custom data dashboard                    | $500 – $5,000+    | 1 – 4 weeks             |
| AI agent prototype                       | $750 – $5,000+    | 2 – 6 weeks             |

These estimates are intentionally wide. Many automation projects look
small but require integration work with vendor-specific tools (POS,
CRM, accounting, hosting). Proposals are tighter than the catalog
ranges.

## Retainers and ongoing support

| Tier                                     | Estimated price        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Monthly support retainer                 | $149 – $799+ / month   |

Retainers cover a defined block of hours, priority response, and
proactive system checks (backups, camera status, site uptime).
Specific terms are written into a short retainer agreement.

---

## Emergency and after-hours pricing

| Surcharge                | Adjustment                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Same-day minimum         | $150                                    |
| After-hours              | +25%                                    |
| Weekend emergency        | +50%                                    |
| Hardware and software    | Billed separately at cost or invoiced   |

After-hours is defined as outside of 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM Eastern.
Weekend emergency is Saturday or Sunday during the same window.

---

## What is not included

- Licensed electrical, alarm, or low-voltage permitted work
- Equipment purchases (billed at cost on a separate line)
- Ongoing software subscriptions after handoff (these are owned by
  the customer)
- Marketing media production (photography, video, copywriting beyond
  what is needed to ship the site)

If a project needs work outside of these ranges, it is quoted in
writing before any work starts.
