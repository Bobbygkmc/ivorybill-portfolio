# Service Catalog

A practical inventory of the services offered through
`dev.uyammadu.com`. This is the source of truth for the website's
`services.html` page. Update here first, then mirror to the page.

Service area: Bucks County, PA and surrounding regions for on-site
work. Remote work is available worldwide.

---

## A. Websites and Digital Presence

For small businesses that need a website that actually works on a phone,
loads quickly, and shows up in local search.

- Landing page design and build
- Multi-page small-business sites
- WordPress repair, cleanup, and modernization
- Local SEO setup and audits
- Google Business Profile cleanup and optimization
- Online ordering flow review and integration support
- Site speed and mobile experience cleanup
- Domain, DNS, and hosting setup

**Typical engagement**: a few hours for a fix, one to five days for a
landing page, one to three weeks for a full small-business build.

---

## B. AI and Automation

Pragmatic automation for small operators. Focused on workflows that
save real hours rather than novelty AI demos.

- AI reporting workflows (daily / weekly summaries)
- Automated business and sales summaries from POS or spreadsheet data
- Lightweight dashboards
- Customer intake and lead-routing automation
- AI agent prototypes for specific business problems
- Local and private AI experiments (running models on owner-controlled
  hardware where appropriate)

This category is positioned as **prototype-grade** unless explicitly
upgraded to a supported deployment. Honesty is the brand here.

Camera and network installation and management. **Not** a licensed
alarm service. We do not market or sell licensed alarm/monitoring
work; only camera, network, NVR, and footage-management services.
See `CAMERA_SYSTEMS_SERVICE_GUIDE.md` for full scope and limits.

- Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) camera installation
- Reolink camera setup
- Frigate / NVR setup and configuration
- Remote viewing configuration (ZeroTier, Tailscale, Reolink app)
- Storage and retention planning
- Footage export and review
- Transfer of ownership and handoff documentation
- Owner and staff training

Restaurant-specific work that pulls from all the categories above.

- Menu digitization

## Engagement model

**Discovery** — short call or visit, scope notes, written estimate.

**Proposal** — fixed estimate or hourly bracket. Hardware and software
costs are billed separately and itemized.

**Delivery** — work performed remotely or on-site. Customer-facing
documentation is provided for any system that the owner is expected to
operate after handoff.

**Handoff** — credentials, ownership transfer, and a written summary
of what was done and how to use it.

**Optional retainer** — for businesses that want ongoing support.

See `PRICING_GUIDE.md` for current price ranges and timeframes.
