How engagements run

Honest scope, in writing, before any hardware ships

Discovery

Short call or visit. We talk through what you actually need vs. what feels urgent. No invoice attached to this conversation.

Proposal

Written estimate with hardware, software, and labor itemized. Not a binding quote until you sign — but tighter than the catalog ranges.

Delivery

Work performed remotely or on-site. You get visibility while it's happening, not just at the end.

Handoff

Credentials transferred to you. Written documentation. Short walkthrough. Optional retainer for ongoing support.
