Home/Services

Fix the systems your business depends on.

I work on websites, cameras, networks, Wi-Fi, POS systems, and restaurant technology in Bucks County. Send the job details for a written estimate.

A. Websites & digital presence

Build a site customers can find and use.

I build small-business websites that work on phones, load quickly, and cover the local searches customers use.

The job is done when customers can find the business and contact it without trouble.

  • Landing page design and build
  • Multi-page small-business sites
  • WordPress repair, cleanup, and modernization
  • Local SEO setup and audits
  • Google Business Profile cleanup and optimization
  • Online ordering flow review and integration support
  • Site speed and mobile experience cleanup
  • Domain, DNS, and hosting setup
B. AI & automation

Automate one clear business task.

I build AI-assisted reports, dashboards, and intake workflows. Each workflow has safety checks and a review trail. AI work is prototype-grade unless the written scope says it has been hardened for production.

Good starting points include a daily sales summary, an inventory alert, or a customer intake form.

  • AI reporting workflows (daily / weekly summaries)
  • Automated business and sales summaries from POS or spreadsheet data
  • Lightweight dashboards
  • Customer intake and lead-routing automation
  • AI agent prototypes for specific business problems
  • Local and private AI experiments on owner-controlled hardware
C. Camera & security systems

Install cameras and keep control of the account.

I install cameras and configure the network and NVR. The owner receives the privileged account and written handoff. Licensed alarm and monitoring services are outside the scope.

See the dedicated cameras page for the full scope, retention planning, and handoff checklist.

Camera systems →
  • PoE camera installation
  • Reolink camera setup
  • Frigate / NVR setup and configuration
  • Remote viewing (Tailscale, ZeroTier, vendor apps)
  • Storage and retention planning
  • Footage export and review
  • Transfer of ownership and handoff documentation
  • Owner and staff training
D. Networking & IT support

Fix the Wi-Fi, printer, or POS.

I troubleshoot the equipment that can stop a shift. That includes Wi-Fi, printers, POS terminals, routers, switches, and small Linux servers.

  • Wi-Fi troubleshooting and coverage planning
  • Router and switch setup
  • Printer troubleshooting (network and USB)
  • POS troubleshooting (Toast, Square, Clover)
  • Raspberry Pi systems and small Linux servers
  • Backups and remote access setup
  • Vendor coordination (ISP, POS provider, hardware vendors)
E. Restaurant technology

Keep the restaurant shift moving.

Restaurant work is my most common engagement. I support POS systems, online ordering, kitchen networks, cameras, and reporting. The owner gets handoff notes.

  • Toast and other POS support
  • Menu digitization
  • Online ordering assistance and integrations
  • Camera and network support sized to a restaurant footprint
  • Inventory and sales dashboards
  • QR-code ordering and table flows
  • Operator handoff documentation
Engagement model

Here is how the job runs.

Discovery

We start with a short call or visit, then I send a written estimate. There is no charge for discovery.

Proposal

Fixed estimate or hourly bracket. Hardware itemized at cost.

Delivery

Remote or on-site. Visibility while the work is happening.

Handoff

You receive the credentials and written handoff. Ongoing support is available if needed.
